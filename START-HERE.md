### 2️⃣ Deploy Online (3 Minutes)

2. Click "New +" → "Web Service"

6. Get your URL: `https://your-game.onrender.com`

## 📊 Game Rules (Quick Reminder)

- **Grid**: 8 rows × 6 columns
- **Players**: 2-4 players take turns
- **Goal**: Be the last player with orbs on the board

**How to play:**
1. Click a cell to place an orb
2. Cells explode at critical mass (2-4 orbs)
3. Explosions spread to adjacent cells
4. Chain reactions can happen!
5. Last player standing wins! 🏆

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**"Can't connect to game"**
- Server running? Check terminal
- Correct URL? Double-check
- Try refreshing the page

**"Game ID not found"**
- Host still in game? They might have left
- Correct ID? Case-sensitive
- Create new game if needed

**"Game already started"**
- Can't join games in progress
- Wait for next game
